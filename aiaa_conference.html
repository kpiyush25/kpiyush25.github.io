AIAA Conference & US Trip Experience

In January 2024, I had the incredible opportunity to attend the American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics (AIAA) SciTech Forum and Exposition in Orlando, Florida. This was my first international conference and my first trip to the United States.

Conference Experience

I presented our paper "Trajectory-Constrained Standoff Target Tracking using Barrier Lyapunov Functions" along with my co-first author. The conference was held at the Hyatt Regency Orlando, Florida from January 8-12, 2024. It was an amazing experience to present our research to an international audience and receive valuable feedback from experts in the field.

US Trip Highlights

Beyond the conference, I had the chance to explore Orlando and experience American culture for the first time. I visited quite a few places, and the Orlando SeaWorld was the most memorable one.
