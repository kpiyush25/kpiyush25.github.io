Meaning in Life

What is the meaning of all you are doing? Does it really matter?

Well, no one knows! It is a philosophical question that has been debated for centuries.

But what I feel is:
Even if life might not have meaning, one can have a few long term purposes, just to make the journey interesting and worthwhile.
Its like passionately playing a video game and having fun, while knowing deep down that it is off reality.
